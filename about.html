Say Hello, to the iPhone 4s

The iPhone 4s was announced on October 4th, 2011 and was released 10 days later. It was the last iPhone to have Steve Jobs as its CEO and the first to have Tim Cook as its CEO later on in development. it was the first iPhone ever to have AI, known as Siri. It was the first iPhone to support iOS 7, the boring flat design update. it Supported iOS 5 to iOS 9.3.6. It was the first iPhone to have a 8MP Backside Camera that can record 1080p at 30fps. It also had a 1.2 MP Frontside Camera that could record at 720p, 30fps. It had a A5 which was a dual core processor with 1GHz. And it was the first iPhone to have that sweet retina display with a 3.5 inch screen and a 960x640 screen resolution, also i consider it the last iphone to have that good everywhere screen size that you could It also had 512mb of DDR2 RAM and a 1430 mAh Li-Ion Battery. It supported HSUPA 4G and HSDPA 4G. It also was the first iPhone to have a antenna signal bar outside of the phone, which on the iPhone 4 was a design flaw, so they fixed it on the iPhone 4s. It was the last iPhone to have the famous 30-pin connector, which was then replaced by the lightning cable on the next iPhone, the iPhone 5. It also was the last iPhone to have that old home button design, which for me was a masterpiece of old school design. It also had a sleek glass back design, which then the iPhone 5 made the back aluminum, which i really dislike. It also was the first iPhone to have the circular volume buttons, which are very nice and smooth.
